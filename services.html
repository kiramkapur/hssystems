    • Our Services
    1. Customized Printed Packaging Consultation (Free Service)

      At H&S Systems, we offer expert consultation on custom printed packaging solutions tailored to your needs. Whether you're launching a new product or rebranding, our specialists guide you through material selection, design choices, and cost-effective printing techniques. We focus on aesthetics, durability, and functionality, ensuring your packaging stands out in the market. This service is completely free, allowing you to explore various options before making a decision. Our commitment to innovation ensures that we help you create packaging that reflects your brand’s identity while maintaining practicality and cost efficiency.

    2. Full-Service Job Contracts with Complete Responsibility

      We provide comprehensive printing and packaging services under a total job contract, where we handle every aspect of production. From design to delivery, H&S Systems takes full responsibility, ensuring seamless execution and on-time completion. Our all-inclusive approach means you don’t have to worry about coordinating multiple vendors—we manage everything, including raw materials, labor, and quality control. Once you place an order, we absorb any fluctuations in material costs, providing you with budget stability.

    3. Offset & Digital Printing Services

      Our printing capabilities cover a wide range of techniques, including offset printing, digital printing, silk-screen printing, flexo printing, gravure printing, and Xerox offset printing. Whether you need high-volume production runs or short-run prints with quick turnaround times, we have the right technology to meet your needs. Our advanced equipment and skilled team guarantee vibrant colors, crisp details, and long-lasting prints.

    4. Specialty Coatings & Finishes

      We offer a variety of premium finishing options to enhance the visual appeal and durability of your printed materials. Our services include lamination, varnishing, UV coating, and special effects like Met Pet, DripOff, foil stamping, and embossing. These finishes not only improve the aesthetics of your packaging but also provide added protection against wear and tear.

    5. Post-Press Fabrication & Binding

      At H&S Systems, we handle all post-press services, including punching, pasting, and a variety of binding options. Whether you need perfect binding for books, wire-o binding for reports, or saddle stitching for brochures, we ensure a professional finish. Our fabrication services also include die-cutting for unique packaging designs and intricate cutouts.

    6. Custom Box & Carton Manufacturing

      We specialize in designing and manufacturing custom boxes and cartons to meet your packaging requirements. From sturdy corrugated boxes to premium board-based cartons, we provide tailored solutions that ensure maximum product protection. Whether you need shipping boxes, display packaging, or branded retail boxes, we offer high-quality materials and structural designs that enhance functionality and aesthetics.

    7. Label & Sticker Printing

      We produce high-quality labels and stickers in various materials, including paper, vinyl, and transparent films. Whether you need product labels, barcode stickers, or decorative decals, our printing technology ensures crisp, fade-resistant designs. We offer customization options such as waterproof coatings, scratch resistance, and strong adhesives.

    8. Promotional & Marketing Material Printing

      Boost your brand visibility with our professionally printed marketing materials, including posters, leaflets, brochures, and letterheads. Our team ensures high-quality prints with vibrant colors and sharp details, making your marketing materials stand out.

    9. Carry Bags & Packaging Accessories

      We manufacture a wide range of carry bags, including paper bags, laminated bags, and eco-friendly alternatives. Our customized carry bags serve as a perfect branding tool for businesses looking to create a lasting impression. Additionally, we provide packaging accessories such as inserts, partitions, and protective layers to enhance product safety during transit.

    10. Calendar & Stationery Printing

      Our services include printing high-quality wall calendars, table calendars, notebooks, and corporate stationery. Whether for promotional use or internal branding, our stationery printing solutions offer a professional touch to your business. We provide custom designs, premium paper options, and a range of binding styles to suit your specific needs.
